# v1.5.4 - The Revamped Weapon Optimizer!!!!

**Released:** 2026-09-12

Following a lot of great feedback on the first version of the Optimizer, it has had some serious rework: it no longer just hands you one "best" build based on your parameters, it now maps out the tradeoffs between Ergonomics, Recoil, and Price as a full curve of generated builds, live, so you can pick the balance that actually fits your playstyle. WOW!

- **Build tradeoff curve** - solves a whole sweep of builds instead of one, plotted as Ergonomics vs. Recoil, Ergonomics vs. Price, or Recoil vs. Price
- **Live progress** - each solve runs in its own isolated worker process, so results stream onto the chart step by step
- **Sampling resolution** - control how many tradeoffs get sampled; equivalent or dominated builds are pruned automatically, so a higher resolution can return more accurate points
- **Interactive chart** - ctrl+scroll to zoom, middle-drag to pan, hover a point for its full stats, click one (or pick from the list) to load its parts straight into the workbench
- All your usual constraints, attachment locks/bans, and market settings carry over from before

## Even More Advanced Advanced Stats!!!!

![The New Advanced Stats Panel](./news/images/v154/advStats.webp "The New Advanced Stats Panel")

The Advanced Stats panel is now a pop-out, organized into sections (General, Camera & Aim, Recoil Pattern, Mounted Fire, Recoil Stabilization, Recoil Recovery), and adds a batch of previously-missing fields: Fire Rate, Camera Recoil, Hand Rotation Damping, Return Path Damping/Offset, and the shot index/angle/step where a weapon's recoil pattern stabilizes.

Special thanks to **[SP-Tushonka 5.0](https://github.com/SP-Tushonka)** for providing the new and updated data behind these stats!

## Also in this update:

- The Optimizer now accounts for magazine ammo weight *while* solving.
- Combo Calculator results transfer a smaller payload and render faster on first load, especially for weapons with a lot of combos.
- Fixed attachment and build ratings occasionally failing to load when a page needed ratings for a large number of items at once.
- Unified how the Recoil % column is calculated and labeled between grid/list view and Combo view.
- Long slot and attachment names that don't fit now marquee-scroll instead of just being cut off.
