# v2.3.0 - Staying on Top of the Game

**Released:** 2026-11-15

Introducing EFTForge v2.3.0, the biggest update since Arcadia v2.0.0.

---

## Stats Straight From Atlas

Until now, every stat on EFTForge came from tarkov.dev. Starting with this update, **item stats come straight from my own service, [Atlas](https://eftforge.com/atlas)**, so they're always fresh on patch day, hidden changes included.

- **Checked every 10 minutes.** When the game updates, EFTForge picks up the new stats on its own, and a notice tells you to refresh.
- **tarkov.dev is still here.** Prices, images, translations, categories and attachment slots still come from tarkov.dev, with complementary data from SP-Tushonka.
- **Same data everywhere.** The builder, the optimizer, the Ballistics Workbench and the Stat Tracker all run on the same numbers.

---

## Test in 3D Range: Now With Recoil!

![Updated 3D Range](./news/images/v230/3Dinspect.webp "Updated 3D Range")

**Sight Picture** is now **Test in Range**. Press it (or **V**) in the 3D builder and your build goes to the range. And now, **you can actually shoot your weapons**!

- **Real recoil.** Every shot kicks with your build's recoil, the way the game does it.
- **Fire modes.** Single, burst and full auto, whatever the weapon has.
- **Stances.** Stand, crouch or go prone. Lower stances drop the view, cut kick and sway, and tire your arms slower, as in the game.
- **Ammo and reloads.** The weapon holds what its magazine takes. Press **R** to reload, twice quickly for the emergency reload, and bolt actions rack the bolt between shots. Or switch on **Infinite Ammo** and never run dry.
- **Sound and animations.** Gunshots and animation sounds, plus a menu to play any of the weapon's animations: reloads, mag and chamber checks, inspect, dry fire and more. Some weapons have no sounds yet.
- **Recoil Overview.** Plots where each shot of a string lands on the target, joined in firing order, with the spread, mean radius, group size and how far the group's center sits from your aim.

### Range Settings

![Range Settings](./news/images/v230/rangeSettings.webp "Range Settings")

The new **Settings** window holds everything in one place:

- **Key bindings** for fire, aim, hold breath, reload, crouch, prone, scope mode and tactical devices. Mouse buttons and two key combos work too.
- **Mouse, aiming and zoom sensitivity**, and **FOV**, each matching your game settings.
- **Skills** (weapon skill, Aim Drills, Strength, Endurance) and **Model Diagnostics**, live graphs of the ADS and recoil models.

## Arena Mode (Testing)

![Arena Mode](./news/images/v230/arenaMode.webp "Arena Mode")

EFTForge now builds for **EFT:Arena**! Use the new **Main / Arena** switch in the header to flip the whole site over to Arena's own data.

Arena mode is still in testing and may have quite a few bugs. If you run into one, please report it on the GitHub issues page!

### Class Loadouts

In Arena, the gun list gives way to a **class picker**. Pick a class and its loadout opens in its own tab, the same way a gun opens a build tab (on mobile, each class keeps one loadout).

![Class Loadouts](./news/images/v230/classLoadout.webp "Class Loadouts")

- **The loadout board.** Weapons, helmet, armor, rig, face cover, eyewear, earpiece and melee, laid out like your in-game equipment screen. Pick a slot to fill it, right click to empty it.
- **Rig and pockets.** Add meds, magazines and ammo, with the cells they take counted against what your rig and pockets hold. Right click an item for Modding, Unload, Duplicate or Discard, or drag it away to throw it out.
- **Meta points.** Everything you carry counts toward the class's meta points, shown against the range the class allows, with a breakdown of where they went.

### Arena Gun Builder

Click **Edit in builder** on a loadout weapon and it opens in the builder you already know, now speaking Arena:

- Prices turn into **meta points**, and **MP/Recoil** replaces ₽/Recoil.
- The **Class** filter hides attachments the class can't use on this weapon.
- A **meta points** breakdown shows how much of the class limit the weapon takes, and how much of it comes from parts (since Arena 0.6.0, parts may make up at most 40% of a preset).
- **Save to loadout** puts the weapon back where it came from.

Save / Share and Explore Builds work on the same saved builds, codes and community builds as the main game. Gun builds can only be published from the main game, since stats can differ between game modes.

### BlastGang Shop

**BlastGang** gets its own card in the class picker, opening the shop settings for **Attacker** and **Defense**: every category with each side's prices, the kit each side starts every round with, and each weapon's build, which you can rework in the builder as long as it stays inside its meta point range and only uses parts BlastGang allows.

![The BlastGang Shop](./news/images/v230/blastGANG.webp "The BlastGang Shop")

### Presets

The loadout page has its own **Save / Share** and **Explore Presets**:

- Save presets per class, share them as preset codes or links, and import someone else's.
- **Publish** a preset to the community straight from your saved list. Community presets show the loadout's board instead of gun stats.
- The header's **Presets** button lists every preset and shop you've saved, plus the ones you've published.
- Only presets with no problems can be published. If a game data update breaks a published preset, it's taken out of the community and you get a notification, so you can fix it and publish it again.

## Skills in the Stats Panel

![Skills in the Stats Panel](./news/images/v230/skillsStats.webp "Skills in the Stats Panel")

The stats panel's strength setting has grown into a **Skills** panel. Set your **weapon skill**, **Strength** and **Endurance**, and the stats panel shows what your build becomes in raid with them, as blue numbers in parentheses next to the normal ones. The normal numbers still leave skills out, so builds keep comparing evenly.

---

## Required Slots

Some weapons can't be equipped in game until certain slots are filled. The 2D builder now knows which ones:

- Required slots are marked in the builder, and a **Not functional** chip shows above the workbench while one is empty. Click it to see which.
- A build with an empty required slot can't be published anymore.
- If a game data update leaves a published build with an empty required slot, it's taken out of the community and you get a notification, so you can fill it and publish it again.

---

## Ballistics Workbench

- **Bullets go on through.** In the armor simulator, a bullet that gets through a limb, the neck or the head can carry on into a spot on another body part, as in the game, which then takes its own hit with whatever damage and pen the bullet has left. The torso and pelvis still stop every bullet. Pick where it goes with the new **Goes on into** option, and the results add the second spot's pen chance, pen damage and HP left.
- **HP left chart.** A new chart view shows each body part's HP left shot by shot.

---

## Other Changes

- **Muzzle smoke.** The attachment table's Loudness column is replaced by **Smoke** and **5° View**, from the 3D viewer's muzzle smoke simulation: how much smoke a muzzle device makes per shot, and the most of your view near the aim point it hides during a string of fire.
- **Optimizer button.** The Optimizer now opens from a button next to the Build and Price views, instead of the tab on the edge of the screen.
- **Desktop app.** The app now pulls game data from eftforge.com every 10 minutes, so it always runs on the same data as the site. Its settings show which game version the data comes from, and **Check for Data Updates** checks for new game data right away, then syncs from tarkov.dev.
