## The Ballistics Workbench

Click the **Ballistics** button in the header and the new ballistics workbench opens as its own tab, right next to your builds! Ballistics Workbench tabs act exactly like how normal build tabs work. On mobile it opens as its own page.

![The Ballistics Workbench](./news/images/v220/ballWorkbench.webp "The Ballistics Workbench")

It has four tools: **Armor simulator**, **Effectiveness**, **Drop table** and **Ammo table**.

### Armor Simulator

Pick a round, dress the target, choose where the bullet lands, and see exactly how the fight plays out, shot by shot.

![Armor Simulator](./news/images/v220/armorSim.webp "Armor Simulator")

- **Loadout panel.** Helmet, helmet attachments, face cover, eyewear, armored rig and body armor, laid out like your in-game equipment screen. Items come with their default plates, and conflicts are checked just like in the game. Helmets show their real icons, drawn by Kitbash!.
- **Target.** Pick the body part and the exact area hit, from the top of the head to the lower back to the left calf. The workbench lists which pieces of your loadout cover that spot, and if a plate only covers part of an area, you choose whether the shot lands on the plate or slips past it.
- **Distance, velocity and angle.** Bullets fly with real drag, so a round slows down on its way to the target and loses damage and pen with it. Velocity mods let you match your gun, and the impact angle lets helmets, visors and some plates ricochet the shot.
- **Results.** Kill chance and armor left per shot, kills on each shot, and a penetration view showing which armor layer stops the bullet and how often. The shot by shot table lists HP left, pen chance and pen damage, and the armor layers name the insert the bullet goes through, with its picture.
- **Compare every round in this caliber.** One click runs the same armor, target, distance and angle for every round in the caliber. Click a row to load it.
- **Bring your build.** The new **Armor Sim** button in the builder opens the simulator with your build's ammo and muzzle velocity already filled in.

All of this runs right on your own computer, in your browser (or the desktop app), and not on the server!!!!

### Effectiveness Chart

![Effectiveness Chart](./news/images/v220/effTable.webp "Effectiveness Chart")

Shots to kill for every round against every armor, worked out ahead of time with the same engine as the armor simulator. Switch between **Body armor** (worn with its default front plate), **Plates** and **Helmets**, filter by caliber or show all of them, and hover a column to see every item it stands for, since items with identical armor share a column.

### Drop Table

Everything about a round's flight, out to whatever distance you like.

![Drop Table](./news/images/v220/dropSim.webp "Drop Table")

- Charts for **Trajectory**, **Speed**, **Damage** and **Penetration**.
- **Zeroing works like the game:** a weapon is zeroed with its default ammo, so other rounds land off the zero. Or switch to zeroing with the round you're shooting.
- **Compare** up to two more rounds of any caliber. Rounds from another caliber get their own zero at the same distance.
- **Accuracy spread** from your weapon's MOA and the round's own accuracy modifier.
- **Scope view.** Click the chart or a table row to look through the scope at that distance, with the reticle ticks in mrad and the target at the right size.
- **Use your build** to fill in its round, velocity mods, accuracy and zero in one click.

### Ammo Table

The ammo table you know now lives in the workbench, with a new **Merge calibers into one table** option to rank every round in the game against each other.
