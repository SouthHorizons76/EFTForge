# v1.6.0 - Kitbash! Image Generation

**Released:** 2026-09-23

This is going to be a big one! EFTForge now draws its own build images with **Kitbash!**, an in-house build image renderer I built from the ground up. No more third party service, no more waiting in queue, no more disclaimers! Plus hidden ammo stats in the Ammo Table and a few other changes!

---

## New Service: Kitbash!

![Kitbash! for EFTForge](./news/images/v160/kitbashForEFTForge_en.png "Kitbash! for EFTForge")

Every custom build image on EFTForge is now drawn by Kitbash!: the live workbench preview, build tab hover previews, optimizer result previews, exported PNGs, and community build cards.

**By the numbers:**

- **Old approach:** preview images came from an outside service through a reverse proxy, and a single request could take up to **10 seconds** (with queues).
- **Kitbash!:** a build image is drawn in **under 50 milliseconds**.
- In the time the old approach took to generate **one** image, Kitbash! can generate **200**.

How it works, in short: every weapon and attachment was rendered once, offline, from the game's own models into a sprite with a depth map. When you change your build, Kitbash! stacks those sprites together at their mount points and depth tests them, so a render needs no game client, no GPU, and no browser. That's why it's fast enough to keep up with you clicking through attachments.

- **Image Generation is on by default again!** Since there's no outside service involved anymore, the disclaimer and checklist you had to go through to turn it on are gone!
- **No more queues!** Renders happen right inside the EFTForge backend, and every rendered build is cached, so revisiting a build is instant.
- **Full magazines.** With **Assume Full Magazine** on, magazines are drawn loaded with your selected ammo, the selected round is drawn in the chamber, and underbarrel grenade launchers are drawn with their round loaded, just like the game, you'll even find the exact same bugs with some specific renders that the game has!
- **New weapons.** Guns tarkov.dev doesn't have an image for yet are now drawn by Kitbash! from their factory preset, or as a bare receiver.
- **Missing parts / Unsupported guns.** If Kitbash! can't draw one of the attachments in your build yet, that part is simply left out of the image companied by a toast message. And if Kitbash! can't draw the whole gun, it instantly errors out instead of having you wait ages for a nothingburger!
- The Generate Previews toggle has gotten a new look!
- The About window now shows which Kitbash! version is running and which EFT game version its sprites were made for.

## Ammo Table: Hidden Stats

The Ammo Table now shows stats the game doesn't tell you in the item description:

- **Pen %** - chance to penetrate
- **Pen Dev** - random deviation applied to penetration power each shot
- **Post-Pen %** - damage retained after penetrating armor
- **Feed Malf %** - chance this round causes a feed malfunction
- **Misfire %** - chance this round misfires
- **Heat** - contribution to weapon overheating per shot
- **Burn** - weapon durability burned per shot, relative to baseline

Rows and columns were made more compact so the wider table fits better, and the table now scrolls both horizontally and vertically on smaller screens.
